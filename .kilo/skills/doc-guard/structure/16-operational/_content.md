# 16-operational

## Operational View - System Operations and Management

**Purpose:** Document how the system is operated, monitored, managed, and supported in production, including procedures, responsibilities, and requirements for operational staff.

The Operational View describes how people run, monitor, and manage the system in production. It provides essential information for operational and support teams to keep the system running, diagnose problems, and perform regular maintenance tasks.

## Key Objectives

This view shall answer the following questions:

* **How is the system operated?** - Daily operational procedures
* **How is the system monitored?** - Monitoring and alerting
* **How are problems diagnosed?** - Troubleshooting procedures
* **How is the system managed?** - Management and administration
* **What are the operational requirements?** - Requirements for operations
* **Who is responsible for what?** - Roles and responsibilities
* **What are the operational procedures?** - Standard procedures
* **How is the system maintained?** - Maintenance and upgrades
